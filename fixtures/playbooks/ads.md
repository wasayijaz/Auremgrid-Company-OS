# Ads playbook

<!-- Playbook metadata: source-neutral operating guidance. -->

Use this before launching or revising paid creative.

1. Read the client brain first.
2. Confirm the current approved offer and price from the evidence layer.
3. Name the decision-maker who can end the ads/marketing/design loop.
4. Do not launch untracked claims. Instrument first.
5. Ship only after Definition of Done is complete.
